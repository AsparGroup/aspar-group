# ASPAR_CONTEXT.md

## Purpose
This file contains the stable context that should survive individual chats, agents and sessions. It is not an operational database.

## Group architecture
ASPAR is an umbrella business system with four commercial capability domains and one shared intelligence/action layer.

### ASPAR Business
Designs and structures investment and business projects: feasibility, market analysis, business model, CAPEX/OPEX, concept, sourcing logic, launch planning and project development.

### ASPAR Building
Turns a project into physical reality: architecture, zoning, 2D/3D, equipment, sourcing, fit-out/works, site execution and opening support.

### ASPAR Solutions
Operates and digitalizes businesses through Odoo/POS/ERP/CRM, inventory, team workflows, automation, marketing workflows, websites and AI-enabled business operations.

### ASPAR Franchise
Makes a proven model duplicable and scalable: audit, economics, SOP, training, governance, franchise development, recruitment and network expansion.

### ASPAR Agent
Shared intelligence/action layer across the four domains. It is not a fifth operating company.

Two capabilities must remain distinct:
- **ASPAR internal delivery/orchestration engine**: the internal stack used to research, design, configure and deliver client systems/projects.
- **ASPAR client-facing Agent**: the bounded interface a client may use to query, analyze, prepare or execute authorized business operations after delivery.

Do not claim these have identical autonomy or capability.

## Majdi personal brand
Majdi Garbouj is a transversal authority/acquisition channel, not a fifth operating company. Personal content may cover business systems, entrepreneurship, AI, Odoo, process design, investment reasoning and ASPAR vision.

## Brand architecture principle
Separation of commercial/editorial routes does not mean four independent infrastructures. The four ASPAR domains share one group vision and can cross-sell when a client need spans multiple stages.

## Core strategic principle
ASPAR should sell outcomes and execution, not internal technical complexity.

Examples:
- Business: from idea/investment question to a project ready to execute.
- Building: from concept to physical opening.
- Solutions: from fragmented operations to an integrated operating system.
- Franchise: from proven business to a governed replicable network.

## Proprietary concepts / Brand Factory
Proprietary concepts are an internal R&D/IP track until they are sufficiently validated. They must not be presented as proven franchises without real operating proof.

The intended maturation path is:
`CONCEPT -> PILOT -> VERIFIED -> DUPLICABLE -> FRANCHISE READY`

## Source-of-truth boundaries
- GitHub Markdown: durable strategy, definitions, decisions, architecture, shared AI context.
- Odoo: operational business records and execution.
- Drive: evidence, files, source documents, archives, heavy assets.
- Canva: design production.
- Blender: 3D production.
- Supabase: optional technical backend when a concrete need requires it.
- Notion: legacy/reference/demo/client-use, not core infrastructure.
- Chats: temporary reasoning only.

## Operating principle
Do not add a new tool unless it replaces an existing function or solves a concrete unmet requirement. Avoid overlapping applications and duplicate sources of truth.
