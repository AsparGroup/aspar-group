# ASPAR Business

## Purpose
Design, study, structure and develop investment/business projects from an initial opportunity or investor objective toward an executable project.

## Current commercial form
ASPAR Business can be sold now as:
- feasibility / investment decision support;
- market and business-model analysis;
- CAPEX/OPEX and scenario structuring;
- concept/project development;
- preparation for execution with Building and/or Solutions when relevant;
- project steering/coordination where explicitly scoped.

Do not require the full proprietary Brand Factory to be finished before selling these services.

## Differentiation
ASPAR Business aims to reduce fragmentation between study and execution. The credible promise is not that ASPAR eliminates every specialist, but that ASPAR can preserve project context and coordinate the path from decision to implementation.

## Data/research principle
A serious study must not be only narrative text. Important conclusions should be traceable to structured inputs such as facts, sources, dates, assumptions, scenarios, calculations and confidence.

Preferred principle:
- databases/structured data record facts, assumptions and history;
- calculation logic derives economics;
- agents research, reason, challenge and orchestrate;
- human review approves consequential conclusions.

## Proprietary concepts / Brand Factory
Status: R&D / HOLD for broad commercialization.

Purpose: detect, filter, score and mature potential concepts/IP.

Do not represent an untested ASPAR-created concept as equivalent in risk to an established franchise.

Maturity path:
`CONCEPT -> PILOT -> VERIFIED -> DUPLICABLE -> FRANCHISE READY`

## Current constraint
ASPAR currently lacks the capital/time to validate many proprietary concepts simultaneously. One strong proof path is more valuable than a large catalog of unvalidated concepts.

## Do not claim
- guaranteed profitability;
- a concept is proven without operating evidence;
- unvalidated financial assumptions as facts;
- a proprietary concept is a franchise-ready asset before it reaches that maturity stage.
