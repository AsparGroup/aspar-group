# ASPAR Building

## Purpose
Turn a business/project concept into a physical environment ready for use or opening.

## Core scope
Depending on project:
- architecture and zoning;
- 2D/3D design;
- equipment and technical requirements;
- sourcing and supplier coordination;
- fit-out / works / site execution;
- budget and execution planning;
- opening support;
- smart-building or connected-operation elements when they solve a real need.

## Relationship with ASPAR Business
Business defines the investment/project logic. Building can consume that context to reduce re-briefing and preserve alignment between business model, space, budget and execution.

## Relationship with ASPAR Solutions
Physical project design should anticipate the operational system where relevant: POS positions, network/equipment needs, staff flow, stock flow, customer journey and digital touchpoints.

## Current commercial status
Sellable opportunistically when a real physical project exists. Building is not required for every ASPAR client.

## Execution principle
AI/3D/Blender can assist design and production, but human validation remains necessary for real-world constructability, budgets, site constraints and execution decisions.

## Do not claim
- fixed CAPEX without validated scope and supplier/site data;
- full automation of architecture/site execution;
- technical compliance or engineering validation outside verified professional scope;
- that every ASPAR Business project must use ASPAR Building.
