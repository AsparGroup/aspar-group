# ASPAR Franchise

## Purpose
Structure, govern and develop a business model that is already sufficiently proven to be duplicated through a network.

## Core scope
Depending on the mandate:
- franchise readiness audit;
- unit economics and franchise economics;
- SOP and operating standards;
- training structure;
- CAPEX/OPEX and opening requirements;
- royalties/fee model analysis;
- franchisee profile and recruitment process;
- governance and network KPI;
- development roadmap;
- coordination with legal/accounting specialists where required.

## Strategic role
Franchise is the scale layer of ASPAR. It should activate when the underlying business has enough evidence and operational structure to justify duplication.

## Relationship with other domains
- Business can help structure the original economic model.
- Building can support future unit rollout where physical execution is required.
- Solutions can provide the operating system and data layer for units/network.
- Agent can assist with bounded reporting, knowledge access and operational workflows.

## Current commercial status
Sell selectively when a real brand/network need exists. Do not force Franchise into projects that are still only concepts.

## Proof principle
A franchise buyer/investor reasonably values operating history, support, systems and evidence. ASPAR must clearly distinguish between a concept, a pilot, a verified unit and a franchise-ready model.

## Do not claim
- franchise readiness without sufficient evidence;
- guaranteed franchisee performance;
- that a new proprietary brand has the risk profile of an established network;
- legal conclusions outside validated professional/legal scope.
