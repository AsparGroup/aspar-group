# ASPAR Solutions

## Purpose
Digitalize and operate businesses through an integrated business system rather than a collection of disconnected tools.

## Core scope
Depending on client need, Solutions may include:
- Odoo/POS;
- CRM/customers;
- inventory and purchasing;
- sales and invoicing;
- team/HR/time-attendance workflows;
- websites and digital workflows;
- marketing/loyalty/campaign workflows;
- reporting and operational dashboards;
- automation and AI-enabled operations;
- ASPAR Agent as a bounded client-facing interface where technically justified.

## Commercial principle
Sell business outcomes, not technical stack complexity.

Examples:
- reduce fragmented manual operations;
- centralize customer/sales/stock/team data;
- configure a business workflow ready for day-to-day use;
- provide a simpler operating interface through automation/AI where useful.

## Current priority
High commercial priority because parts of the stack can already be demonstrated and delivered without waiting for the full long-term ASPAR architecture.

## Relationship with Odoo
Odoo is the intended operational center for business execution where it fits the client case. ASPAR Solutions configures and integrates the business workflow around the client's real operating model.

## Pricing status
Do not invent or publish unvalidated pricing, setup fees, subscription economics or margins. Pricing and contribution margin must be validated against real delivery costs.

## Do not claim
- that every module is production-ready for every client;
- that ASPAR Agent has unlimited autonomy;
- that internal ASPAR delivery capabilities are identical to the client-facing agent;
- guaranteed savings or revenue increases without measured evidence.
